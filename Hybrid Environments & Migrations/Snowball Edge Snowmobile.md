
## Personal Summary

- The Snowball Series are designed to move large sizes of data IN and OUT of AWS. 
- Physical storage - suitcase or truck
- Ordered from AWS Empty, Load up, Return
- Ordered from AWS with data, empty & return

- Ordered from AWS, Log a Job, Device Delivered to you (not instant)
- Data Encryption using KMS
- 50TB  or 80 TB Capacity
- 1  Gbps  or 10 Gbps Network (physical networking is required)
- Economical range of using Snowball is  within the range of  10 TB to 10 PB (multiple devices)
- You can have multiple devices (80 TB max) delivered to multiple business premises.


- Snowball Edge: Both Storage and Compute
- Larger Capacity vs Snowball
- Faster Networking: 10Gbps, 10/25 (SFP), 40/50/100 Gbps
- Storage Optimized- (With EC2) 80TB, 24 vCPU, 32 GiB RAM. 1 TB SSD
- Compute Optimized - 100TB + 7.68 NVME, 52 vCPU and 208 GiB RAM
- Compute with GPU - As above, with a GPU
- Ideal for remote sites or where data processing on ingestion is needed

- Snowmobile: Portable Data Center within a shipping container on a LITERAL TRUCK
- Special order
- Ideal for a single location when 10 PB+ is required
- Up to 100PB per snowmobile
- Not economical for multi-site (unless huge) or sub 10 PB
- One Location ONLY, NO ROADTRIPS TO MULTIPLE SITES

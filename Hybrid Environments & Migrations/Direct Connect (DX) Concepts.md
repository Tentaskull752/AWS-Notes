
## Personal Summary

- A physical connection into a AWS Region (1, 10, or 100 Gbps)
- Business premises -> DX Location -> AWS Region
- You're ordering a Port Allocation at a DX Location
- Port Hourly Cost & Outbound Data Transfer
- Provisioning Time... physical cables & no resilience
- Low Latency & consist latency + High Speeds
- AWS Private Services (VPCs) and AWS Public Services (NO INTERNET)
- DX Connect Location has two cages within it: - AWS Direct Connect Cage, contains a AWS DX Router(s) which are DX Endpoints. Customer/Comms Partner Cage contain Optional Infrastructure with a Customer/Partner "DX Router"
- Order a Cross Connect; a direct connection between AWS Port and your router (or communication partner router) within the DX location

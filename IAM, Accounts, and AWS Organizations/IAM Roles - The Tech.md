  - A Role can be used by an unknown number of principals, whether that be people, services, or applications within your outside of your account.
  - Temporary-basis; a role doesn't represent you, but a level of access within the AWS account; you borrow the permissions for a limited amount of time. You become that role.
  - IAM Roles can have policies; trust and permissions policies. Trust controls what identities can assume that role. If Identity A is allowed in the trust policy, then Identity A can become that role. If a role is assumed by something that is allowed to, temporary Security Credentials are temporary security passes given to the identity assuming that role. And once logged in, the permissions that the identity is scaled against determined what the person in that role can do.


#### Personal Summary 

 - Service-linked roles are IAM roles that are linked to a specific AWS service. 
 - Permissions are predefined by the service. 
 - They provide permissions that a service needs to interact with other services on your behalf.
 - Service might create/delete the role, or allow you to during the setup or within IAM. 
 - Can't delete role until it's not longer required.  
 - Role-separation: 

 - For "Bob" to access a role connect to a service, but not allow creation, destruction, or altering of that role. PassRole Permissions are applied.

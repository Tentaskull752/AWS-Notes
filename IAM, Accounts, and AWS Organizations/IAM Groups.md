
#### Personal Summary 

 - IAM Groups are containers for users. You can't log into AMI Groups and they have no credentials. TEST WILL TEST YOU.
 - Groups can have policies attached to them, inline and management policies. 
 - There is no limit for the number of users within IAM Groups. 
 - User-only groups do not exists naturally, and there is NO Group Nesting. 
 - Limit of 300 groups per account, can be increased w/ ticket. 
 - Resources such as a bucket, can reference identities within their own resource policy. 
 - Groups are not a true identity, and can't be referenced as a principal in a policy.

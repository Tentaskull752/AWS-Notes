
## Personal Summary

- Price Class - determines what Edge Locations distributions are deployed to. 
- Can choose the Security Policy at the Distribution Level. Have to pick the one with the best security and accessibility for your users. 
- Distributions can operate at both Ipv4 and Ipv6.
- Distiributions CAN HAVE MULTIPLE BEHAVIORS.
- TO RESTRICT VIEWER ACCES: Can select behavior to be restricted or private. Have to specify trusted Key Groups or Signers (Legacy method). Need Sign-cookers or Sign-url's to access this restricted content. 
- Can compress content automatically within Behavior settings.

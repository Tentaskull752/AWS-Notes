
### Personal Summary

Region: A region cluster of Availability zone's; and these can be one data center or more.

Availability Zone: The level below a region. Has lowest resiliency between Global, regional, and AZ. A region can have multiple AZ, but if one fails, any service within it will fail as well.

Global: global services provided, ex. IAM and 53. Would have to take the world to go out for these to stop functioning. Most resilient (globally resilient).

Latency can be affected by the distance of which a service has to travel from the hardware in a data storage center to the user; the closer, the better.

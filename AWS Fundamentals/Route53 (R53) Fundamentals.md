
### Personal Summary

 - Route 53 is a global service that is globally resistant, with nodes in each region and is connected to a single database.
 - Registries contains zones; zones being for ex., .org and .com and .net.
 - Once a domain is registered in the registry to it's designated zone (ex. dogs.com goes to .com)
 - The top level domain in the .com zone is checked to make sure that domain is not taken.
 - Once accepted, the domain is given a zone file, which is stored on 4 separate nameservers. 
 - Can be public or private if you connect the nameservers to a VPC(s)
 - These nameservers store records, and those are called recordsets.

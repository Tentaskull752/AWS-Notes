
### Personal Summary
High-Availability (HA): 
Keeping systems online as long as possible, and allowing the fixing of those systems in the shortest and most affordable method as possible. 

Availability can come in at the value of 99.9%, 99.99%, and 99.999%; with each amount of downtime exponentially decreasing with each increase in percentage. It's about fast and efficient recovery when there is a failure, not prevention. 

 - "A 4x4 meant for replacement and getting back on the road"

Fault-Tolerance (FT):
The property of a system being capable of continuing operation after the failure of a singular or multiple components within the system. It is a very expensive compared to (HA)

 - "The jet engines of a plane, when one goes down, the other 3 keep the plane flying"

Disaster-Recovery (DR):
Tools and procedures to enable the recovery or continuation of vital technology infrastructure and systems.
Need plans in place; backups are essential. If something goes down, you back up the resource to off-site storage in a standby location. 

 - "The parachute when the plane is going down and saves your life"
